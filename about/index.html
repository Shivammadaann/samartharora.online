About Samarth

A contemporary Indian voice, bringing Hindi-Punjabi stories to life through melody, mood, and honest songwriting.

His story

From feeling to song

Samarth is a Delhi-based singer-songwriter and composer creating a sound that moves naturally between Hindi and Punjabi expression.

His music begins with emotion: a line that lingers, a melody that arrives unexpectedly, or a thought that needs somewhere to live. The result is intimate, contemporary music with space for the listener to find their own story.

His debut track, “DUA,” arrived in late 2022 and introduced a soothing Hindi sound shaped by restraint and sincerity. It marked the opening chapter of a catalogue built around feeling rather than formula.

As Samarth continues to write and compose, the focus stays simple—make honest music, explore new textures, and create songs worth returning to.
